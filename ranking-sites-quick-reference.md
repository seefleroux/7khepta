## Common Ranking Criteria

### Performance-Based
- ✅ Direct time/distance/height comparison
- ✅ Simple and transparent
- ✅ Wind-legal vs. wind-aided distinctions
- ⚠️ Doesn't consider strength of competition
- ⚠️ May not reflect "best" athlete, just fastest performance

### Points-Based (IAAF Tables)
- ✅ Allows cross-event comparison
- ✅ Scientific/statistical basis
- ✅ Used for multi-events
- ⚠️ Complex for general understanding
- ⚠️ May not reflect event-specific competition

### Power Ratings (Athletic.net)
- ✅ Considers competition strength
- ✅ Rewards consistency
- ✅ More predictive of championship success
- ⚠️ Proprietary algorithm (less transparent)
- ⚠️ Requires comprehensive data

## Results Submission Best Practices

### Before Meet
- ✅ Pre-register on ranking platform
- ✅ Verify athlete names and information
- ✅ Ensure meet is certified/recognized
- ✅ Confirm timing/measurement standards

### During Meet
- ✅ Record accurate wind readings
- ✅ Note timing method (FAT vs. hand)
- ✅ Document any irregularities
- ✅ Collect official results

### After Meet
- ✅ Submit results within 48 hours
- ✅ Verify all performances uploaded correctly
- ✅ Check for errors or duplicates
- ✅ Add any missing information
- ✅ Monitor ranking updates

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Data Quality
- ❌ Submitting results without verification
- ❌ Incorrect wind readings (affects legal status)
- ❌ Wrong timing method designation
- ❌ Duplicate entries for same performance
- ❌ Missing athlete/team affiliations

### Strategic Errors
- ❌ Focusing only on rankings, ignoring development
- ❌ Over-competing for ranking points
- ❌ Comparing rankings across different systems
- ❌ Ignoring ranking methodology differences
- ❌ Setting unrealistic ranking goals

### Administrative Issues
- ❌ Inconsistent results submission
- ❌ Not claiming/verifying team profiles
- ❌ Allowing outdated information to persist
- ❌ Not monitoring rankings regularly
- ❌ Missing correction deadlines

## Reading Rankings: What the Numbers Mean

### Ranking Position
```
#1 in State = Elite (college recruitment level)
#1-10 in State = High performing (strong college prospect)
#11-25 in State = Very competitive (college consideration)
#26-50 in State = Competitive (regional recognition)
#51-100 in State = Developing competitor
#100+ in State = Participation/early development
```

### Team Rankings
```
Top 5 = Elite program
Top 10 = Strong established program
Top 25 = Competitive program
Top 50 = Solid program with competitive presence
Lower = Developing or participation-focused program
```

### Power Ratings (Athletic.net scale)
```
1000+ = Elite national level
900-999 = Strong national/regional elite
800-899 = State championship competitive
700-799 = State competitive
600-699 = Regional competitive
<600 = Developing
```

## FAQ

**Q: How often are rankings updated?**
A: Most platforms update daily during competitive season, less frequently in off-season.

**Q: Do all meets count for rankings?**
A: No—typically only certified meets with proper timing/measurement standards count.

**Q: What if there's an error in the rankings?**
A: Contact the platform's support team with documentation to request correction.

**Q: Can rankings hurt recruiting chances?**
A: Rankings are one of many factors. Strong improvement trends can be as valuable as high rankings.

**Q: Should we enter athletes in events just for ranking opportunities?**
A: No—athlete development and long-term success should guide event selection.

**Q: How do we balance rankings with participation goals?**
A: Set different goals for different athlete tiers; celebrate achievements at all levels.

**Q: Are wind-aided performances ranked?**
A: They may be listed separately but typically don't count for official rankings in affected events.

**Q: How important are rankings for college recruiting?**
A: Very helpful for visibility, but coaches also value improvement trends, work ethic, and character.
